CleanLock Privacy Policy

We collect no data, and the app does not connect to the internet.

CleanLock has no accounts, analytics or advertising. Your settings stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

The Microsoft Store handles the free trial, payment and updates under Microsoft's own privacy terms. We receive no personal information from CleanLock.

Last updated: November 2026
